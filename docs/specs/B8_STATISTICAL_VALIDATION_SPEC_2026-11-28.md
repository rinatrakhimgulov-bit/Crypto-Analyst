# B8 Statistical Validation Specification
Date: 2026-11-28
Status: Draft for implementation
Base: B7 candidate branch `feature/b7-setup-engine-2026-11-28`, head `46820a743fc1c08eb34b6c1851315d8b57ef8e1d`

## 1. Purpose

B8 measures whether the research candidates emitted by B7 are followed by statistically distinguishable, directionally consistent price behavior. It also establishes a prospective evaluation path using immutable B5 journal observations.

B8 is an evaluation layer. It must not change B1/B2/B7 outputs, the current LONG/SHORT/WAIT decision path, or the user's trading behavior.

The central question is not "does the app make money?" It is:
- how often does each setup precede favorable versus adverse movement;
- how large are forward returns, maximum favorable excursion (MFE) and maximum adverse excursion (MAE);
- how often is the setup invalidated before reaching a declared target;
- how sensitive are results to entry delay, outcome horizon and explicit cost assumptions;
- do results persist on chronological out-of-sample data and prospective journal observations?

## 2. Hard boundaries

- No changes to the current LONG/SHORT/WAIT engine.
- No B2-F directional gate and no MTF-derived direction until its live provider-time contract is calibrated.
- No confidence percentage, win-probability label or "accuracy" badge in the production UI.
- No automatic execution, leverage, position sizing or trade recommendations.
- No claim of profitability from setup hit rate, historical event counts, or a small sample.
- No random train/test split. All evaluation splits are chronological.
- No future candle, future pivot, future zone or later B2/B7 state may affect a candidate or its entry timestamp.
- Historical research candidates and live B5 observations are separate evidence populations. They must never be silently pooled.
- Actual trades are separate from paper observations and setup candidates. A LONG/SHORT observation is not a trade.
- Unknown fees, funding, slippage or execution details remain unknown; they must not be silently set to zero and described as net profitability.

## 3. Evidence populations

### 3.1 Historical research population

Generated by replaying the pinned OHLCV archive through the same B2 and B7 source modules, at explicit historical cutoffs.

Every candidate must preserve:
- symbol, market type and timeframe;
- setup ID/type/direction/status;
- B7 policy and engine versions;
- snapshot ID and analysis cutoff;
- candidate `available_at_ms`;
- evidence references and invalidation level;
- dataset repository, immutable dataset commit and row-range provenance.

Deduplicate by stable candidate identity within each symbol/timeframe series. Repeated appearances across overlapping replay windows are not independent observations and must not inflate sample size.

The existing 30-window B7 replay (40 candidates) is a causal smoke/replay sample only. It is not a complete event census, a backtest or an outcome sample. B8 must build a separately versioned candidate dataset with explicit replay cadence and deduplication.

### 3.2 Prospective B5 journal population

B5 exports a versioned JSON envelope containing immutable observations, append-only follow-ups and optional actual trades.

B8 import must:
- accept only supported journal format/version and validate the full envelope before processing;
- preserve original records and IDs; never rewrite or enrich an observation in place;
- read the captured B7 output only from the original `analysisSnapshot.b2` payload when present and version-compatible;
- keep LONG, SHORT, WAIT, quality-blocked WAIT, pending setup, missed setup and no-setup observations in the denominator/reporting;
- preserve capturedAt and candidate available-at timestamps;
- report missing B7 snapshots, unsupported versions and incomplete candle windows as exclusions with explicit reason codes;
- never infer that an observation was traded.

The user must explicitly export and provide the B5 JSON. No background upload, backend or telemetry is introduced.

### 3.3 Actual execution population

Manually entered B5 `actual_trade` records are evaluated separately. Report only what the record actually contains. Missing quantity, fees, funding, fills or timestamps must remain missing. Actual trade statistics must not be blended with simulated historical candidate outcomes.

## 4. Event and outcome semantics

### 4.1 Candidate event

A candidate becomes eligible for evaluation only when:
- B7 status is `CONFIRMED`;
- B1/B2/B7 snapshot, symbol, timeframe and cutoff bindings are valid;
- `available_at_ms` is a safe integer and not later than the source analysis cutoff plus the declared finality semantics;
- candidate evidence is reproducible from the pinned prefix;
- the candidate has not already been counted by its stable identity.

`PENDING`, `EXPIRED`, `BLOCKED` and B1 quality-blocked observations are reported as coverage/diagnostic counts, not confirmed setup outcomes.

### 4.2 Causal entry proxy

The historical OHLCV archive does not contain tick-level executable quotes or order-book fills. Therefore B8 must not pretend to model an exact market fill.

Baseline research entry:
- identify the first fully closed candle whose close availability (`close_time_ms + finality_safety_lag_ms`) is strictly later than the candidate's `available_at_ms`;
- use that candle's close as the declared bar-close entry proxy;
- record entry delay in milliseconds and bars;
- begin barrier/outcome evaluation only on candles after that entry candle;
- exclude candidates with no eligible future entry candle or insufficient forward horizon, with explicit reasons.

This deliberately avoids using the next candle's open when that open occurred before the candidate was actually available. A separately named sensitivity mode may test alternative delays, but may not replace or be mixed with the baseline.

### 4.3 Directional forward outcomes

For each confirmed candidate, calculate direction-adjusted forward return and excursion over fixed horizons of 1, 3, 6 and 12 bars (or mark a horizon unavailable if the series ends first).

For LONG:
- favorable excursion uses future high relative to entry;
- adverse excursion uses future low relative to entry.

For SHORT:
- favorable excursion uses entry relative to future low;
- adverse excursion uses entry relative to future high.

Report:
- forward close return;
- MFE and MAE;
- whether the declared invalidation level was touched;
- time/bars to first invalidation touch;
- whether a target barrier was reached only when a target policy is explicitly selected.

Do not label a positive forward return as a winning trade.

### 4.4 Optional barrier simulation

A secondary, explicitly parameterized research mode may use:
- stop = B7 candidate `invalidation_level`;
- target = entry-to-stop risk distance multiplied by a fixed R multiple (at minimum 1R and 2R);
- a target/stop touch is evaluated only after the causal entry proxy;
- if stop and target are both inside the same OHLC candle and intrabar ordering is unknown, resolve conservatively as stop-first and flag `AMBIGUOUS_SAME_BAR`;
- if the candle opens beyond the stop, use the open as the adverse stop proxy; do not assume a better fill;
- if the candle opens beyond target, cap the target proxy at the declared target price;
- candidates with missing/invalid stop distance are not barrier-scored.

Barrier outcomes are research simulations, not fills and not evidence of executable profitability.

## 5. Costs and funding

Every cost assumption must be stored in a versioned configuration and echoed in the report:
- entry fee bps;
- exit fee bps;
- entry slippage bps;
- exit slippage bps;
- funding treatment: observed funding series, explicit scenario assumption, or `UNKNOWN`.

Provide at least:
1. gross price-movement results;
2. illustrative cost-sensitivity results under named assumptions;
3. net-of-known-cost results only where all required cost inputs are present.

If funding is unavailable, mark funding as unknown and do not call the result fully net. Do not select one "realistic" fee tier without a known account/venue fee schedule. Sensitivity assumptions are research parameters, not user-specific trading costs.

## 6. Bias, dependence and sample integrity

- Use chronological splits per symbol/timeframe: initial train, validation and untouched test periods. The split boundaries and row timestamps must be written into the report before reading test outcomes.
- If parameters are tuned, tune only on train/validation. Freeze policy and thresholds before test.
- Prefer walk-forward evaluation with expanding train and forward test blocks when sample size permits.
- Do not randomly shuffle bars or candidates.
- Count unique candidate IDs, not overlapping replay appearances.
- Report candidate overlap and clustering by symbol, timeframe, date/week and setup type.
- Correlated candidates from the same market move are not independent samples. Confidence intervals must use a block/cluster-aware method where feasible; otherwise clearly label naive intervals as optimistic.
- Report sample size and uncertainty beside every rate/mean. Include Wilson intervals for simple proportions and bootstrap intervals for return/excursion summaries only when the resampling unit is defensible.
- Report missing/excluded observations and their reason codes. Never silently discard WAIT/no-setup or data-quality failures when reporting system coverage.
- No multiple-threshold fishing on the final test period. If many variants are explored, disclose the number of variants and treat the result as exploratory.

## 7. Required metrics

For each setup type, direction, symbol, timeframe, split and cost scenario, report:

**Coverage and data quality**
- total observations/candidate events;
- confirmed, pending, expired, blocked and no-setup counts;
- evaluable and excluded counts with reasons;
- unique IDs and duplicate appearances;
- entry-delay distribution and horizon coverage.

**Directional behavior**
- forward-return distribution by horizon;
- MFE and MAE distribution;
- invalidation-touch rate and time-to-invalidation;
- target-touch rate only for a declared target policy;
- favorable/adverse excursion ratio with uncertainty.

**Barrier research simulation**
- stop-first / target-first / timeout / ambiguous counts;
- gross R distribution;
- cost-adjusted R under each explicit scenario;
- maximum adverse excursion and drawdown of a clearly defined sequential, non-overlapping hypothetical stream only. Do not calculate portfolio drawdown by summing overlapping candidate events as if all were simultaneously tradable.

**Statistical reliability**
- sample size;
- confidence intervals;
- chronological split results;
- sensitivity to entry delay, horizon and cost assumptions;
- concentration by symbol/timeframe/regime;
- stability between train, validation, test and prospective journal.

No single aggregate score may hide poor performance in a particular symbol/timeframe or setup family.

## 8. B8 architecture and files

Keep the evaluator independent from production UI and decision code.

Proposed modules:
- `src/research/b8-outcome-evaluator.js`: pure deterministic outcome and barrier evaluation.
- `src/research/b8-statistics.js`: summary metrics and interval calculations.
- `tools/b8_historical_validation.cjs`: pinned archive replay, candidate deduplication, chronological split and report generation.
- `tools/b8_journal_validation.cjs`: validation of an explicitly supplied B5 export; no automatic access to browser localStorage.
- `tests/b8-outcome-evaluator.test.cjs`
- `tests/b8-statistics.test.cjs`
- `tests/b8-historical-validation.test.cjs`
- `docs/audits/B8_HISTORICAL_VALIDATION_YYYY-MM-DD.md`

The initial B8 release is research-only. Do not add a production dashboard or change the current UI. A minimal report artifact is sufficient.

## 9. Required deterministic tests

At minimum:
- candidate available after candle open cannot enter at that earlier open;
- first eligible close entry is selected deterministically;
- no future candle before candidate availability affects the result;
- LONG/SHORT directional return and MFE/MAE mirror correctly;
- incomplete horizon is marked unavailable, not zero;
- stop-only, target-only, timeout and same-bar ambiguous barrier cases;
- conservative stop-first handling for same-bar ambiguity;
- gap-through-stop uses adverse open proxy;
- invalid/missing invalidation level excludes barrier simulation but may retain forward-return metrics;
- duplicate candidate appearances count once;
- same event across overlapping windows does not inflate N;
- chronological split prevents train/test overlap;
- parameter tuning never reads test outcomes;
- journal WAIT/no-setup/quality-blocked records remain visible in coverage;
- actual trades are reported separately from simulated candidates;
- unknown funding/costs never silently become zero;
- malformed journal import is rejected without modifying source records;
- deterministic repeatability and stable report ordering.

## 10. Acceptance gates

B8 is not complete merely because a script runs.

**B8-A — methodology and data contract**
- This specification reviewed against actual B5/B7 schemas.
- Entry, outcome, cost, split, deduplication and uncertainty semantics fixed.
- Known historical archive limitations documented.

**B8-B — deterministic evaluator**
- Pure modules and unit tests pass.
- Causal entry and outcome calculations independently checked with hand-calculated fixtures.

**B8-C — historical validation**
- Candidate population is generated from a pinned dataset with deduplication and exact provenance.
- Chronological train/validation/test or walk-forward report produced.
- Full metrics and exclusions published.
- No profitability claim unless a separately justified, non-overlapping execution simulation with complete cost inputs supports it.

**B8-D — prospective journal path**
- Explicit B5 export validates and imports without mutation.
- Paper observations, WAIT/no-setup, follow-ups and actual trades remain distinct.
- Prospective results are reported separately and only after enough forward observations accumulate.

**B8-E — independent audit**
- Source review of causal timing, split boundaries, leakage, dependence, arithmetic and cost treatment.
- Exact-head CI passes.
- No integration into LONG/SHORT/WAIT until a later separately approved stage.

## 11. Known constraints at opening

- B7 historical replay currently covers 30 rolling 160-candle windows and 18 prefix checks, yielding 40 candidate appearances (36 confirmed, 4 pending). These are not yet deduplicated outcome events and do not establish a win rate.
- The current replay uses a synthetic B1-VALID wrapper and fixed 500ms time-derived finality; it does not execute the real B1 validator.
- The pinned archive ends on 2025-12-05.
- No tick-level fills, spread, funding history, account fee tier, slippage observations or actual trade outcomes are present in that archive.
- B5 journal is browser-local. The repository cannot inspect the user's private localStorage; prospective validation requires an explicit user-exported JSON file.
- The trend-continuation setup has not appeared in the 30 existing replay windows; synthetic unit tests alone do not establish historical frequency or performance.

These limitations must remain visible in every B8 report until independently resolved.
